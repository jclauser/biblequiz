Voice and input

Microphone check

Chrome and Edge work best. They send microphone audio to an online speech service, so you need an internet connection, and the page must be served over HTTPS or from localhost. On an iPhone, turn off Silent Mode and raise the volume to hear the questions.

Question bank

Fields: ref, type (Quotation, Completion, Direct, MultiPart) and text (the verse). Optional: question and answer (Direct), question and answers array (MultiPart), preamble. Direct and MultiPart questions need an answer/answers value, otherwise they are graded against the whole verse. Completion questions always read the first five words of the verse.

Rules used by this trainer
  • Questions keep coming until you tap End match. There is no question limit and no time limit.
  • Tap Jump to stop the reading, then answer out loud.
  • Your answer is submitted after a short pause in your speaking, or when you tap Submit answer.
  • Questions are read the FEC way: the question number, then \u201cQuestion.\u201d Each is announced as a regular or reference question (reference questions begin \u201cAccording to \u2026\u201d). The question is read once (use Repeat question for practice).
  • A question that starts with \u201cAccording to\u201d is a reference question; any other direct question is regular. Finish-this-verse is asked two ways: First Words (the first five words are read; finish the verse) or Quote (the reference is read; say the whole verse). Both must be word-perfect.
  • Scriptural questions are worded exactly as in Scripture and need the word-perfect answer. Situation questions are announced with the items to give (for example, who said it, to whom) and do not need to be word-perfect.
  • Quotations must be verbatim. Extra or wrong words are errors.
  • For regular, reference and situation answers, you need most of the key words. Extra words that are not part of the answer count against you: a few make it a close call for you to rule on, and many make it an error.
  • Use Repeat question to hear a question again, or Skip and show answer to see it.
